Privacy

How Cancer Innovate handles your data.

Updated 15 June 2026. This page restates the organisation’s published approach in clearer language.

In short

No selling data

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

In short

Minimal collection

We only collect what you choose to give us — and this site sets no analytics cookies.

In short

Your rights

Ask to access, correct, or delete your information any time: hello@cancerinnovate.com.

Cancer Innovate (“we”) protects personal information and uses it only for the reasons below when you use this website.

What we collect

If you contact us, join a list, ask about a programme, or take part in a survey, event, or community activity, you may give us your name, email, phone number, organisation, and whatever you choose to write in a message.

How it is used

We use that information to reply, tell you about programmes and events that match your request, improve the site, and keep it secure. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The public site may use cookies and similar tools that record browser type, device, IP address, pages visited, and time on the site, mainly to understand performance. This rebuilt preview does not set analytics cookies.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers who help run the site or our activities, under a duty to keep it confidential, or when the law requires disclosure.

Security and your rights

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures. No internet transmission is perfectly secure. Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your information, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent. Email hello@cancerinnovate.com.

Children and other sites

This website is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Links to other sites, including 90 Seconds and social networks, follow those organisations’ own policies.

Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can go to hello@cancerinnovate.com or via the contact page. Website: cancerinnovate.com.
