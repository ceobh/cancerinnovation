Questions, answered

Innovation Hub FAQ.

Who can apply to the Innovation Hub?

Founders and teams working on ideas that improve cancer screening, diagnosis or the treatment experience. Stage matters less than ambition and evidence.

Does it cost anything to join?

Conversations start free. Specific programmes, space and support depend on the fit — write to us and we will outline the routes available.

Do I need to be based in Birmingham?

No. The hub is rooted in the Midlands but works with founders and partners across the UK and internationally.

What kind of support is on offer?

Mentorship, introductions to clinicians and researchers, funding pathway guidance, workshops, and access to the wider Cancer Innovate community.
