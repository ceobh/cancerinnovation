Who we are

A home for cancer innovation in the Midlands.

Cancer Innovate brings healthcare, technology, research, and industry into one place: an innovation hub with a dedicated research centre, based in Birmingham.

The mission

Accelerate the development of non-invasive early cancer screening. Do it through collaboration, not isolated projects. Use innovation, artificial intelligence, and quantum computing where they can shorten the path from idea to something patients can actually use.

The organisation exists to convene people who rarely share a room: founders, clinicians, researchers, patients, and partners who want their work tied to outcomes.

How the work is organised

  • Innovation HubSpace, mentorship, and routes into funding and clinical settings for new ideas.
  • Research CentrePartnerships between clinicians and researchers, with patients in the design of the work.
  • CommunityA public way in for entrepreneurs, patients, clinicians, researchers, and sponsors.
