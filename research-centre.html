Research Centre

Research organised around earlier detection.

The centre connects clinicians and researchers so screening ideas move with clinical reality attached, and with patients involved in how solutions are shaped.

Research partnerships

Clinicians and researchers in the same conversation, aimed at breakthroughs in how cancer is found earlier and with less burden on the person being screened.

Technology and data

Artificial intelligence and, where it earns its place, quantum computing — used to change diagnostics, not as a slogan beside them.

Patient engagement

People with lived experience help decide what “better” means: clearer screening, less invasive tests, and care that respects daily life.

Impact

The centre’s measure of success is improved outcomes and lives changed, not the number of meetings held.

Questions, answered

Research Centre FAQ.

What research does the centre support?

Work aimed at non-invasive early cancer screening — from new biomarkers and imaging to AI and quantum methods that make detection earlier and gentler.

How are patients involved?

People with lived experience help define what a better test looks like — shaping studies so the end result works in real life, not just in papers.

Can external researchers collaborate?

Yes. Academic groups, NHS teams and technology builders can propose a collaboration through the contact page.

Is this connected to the Innovation Hub?

Directly. The centre provides the scientific rigour; the hub provides the route for proven ideas to reach patients and partners.
